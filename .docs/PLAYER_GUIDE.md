# Player Guide: Survival Manual (PLAYER_GUIDE.md)

Welcome to the abyss. This living manual outlines everything you need to know to survive the infinite depths of the dungeon.

## 1. The Core Loop & The Timer

Your goal is simple: level up, acquire gear strong enough to keep you alive, and find the stairs before time runs out.

- **The Global Timer:** Every floor features a strict 5-minute time limit. There are no extensions.
- **Zero-Second Permadeath:** If the clock strikes exactly 0:00, your run is over. You die instantly. Death is permanent; there is no progression carryover between runs.
- **Pausing:** The timer and all enemies stop *only* when you have a menu open (Inventory, Skills, Stats). Use this time to catch your breath, analyze your loot, and plan your build.
- **The Stairs:** You must find the staircase within the massive 5-minute window. Descending resets the timer to 5 minutes, but the enemies on the next floor will be significantly stronger.

## 2. Controls & Interactions

- **WASD / Arrow Keys:** Move character.
- **Left Mouse Button / Spacebar:** Attack / Fire Weapon.
- **E:** Interact (Loot items from the floor, talk to vendors, use stairs).
- **I:** Open Inventory (Pauses game).
- **C:** Open Character Stats & Skills (Pauses game).

## 3. Combat Archetypes

The game supports highly flexible weapon types. Your combat playstyle is entirely dictated by what you choose to equip.

| Weapon Archetype | Tactical Description | Pros & Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Melee (Swords/Axes)** | Close-quarters combat with wide sweeping arcs. | High base damage, but puts you in extreme danger of taking hits. |
| **Ranged (Bows/Guns)** | Projectile-based attacks fired from a distance. | Keeps you safe, but lower base damage. Highly reliant on movement speed. |
| **Magic (Staves/Tomes)** | Casts spells that apply chaotic elemental effects. | Huge area-of-effect potential, but often requires standing still to cast. |

## 4. Loot, Rarities, and Affixes

Armor provides the "Armor" stat (reduces incoming damage). Weapons provide the "Damage" stat. Loot is color-coded by rarity. Finding higher rarity gear is critical for survival because it drastically multiplies the base stats of the item and adds **Affixes**.

- **Common (White):** 0 Affixes. Just raw stats.
- **Uncommon (Green):** 1 Affix.
- **Rare (Blue):** 2 Affixes.
- **Epic (Purple):** 3 Affixes.
- **Legendary (Gold):** 4 Affixes.

*What are Affixes?* Affixes are game-changing magical effects. You might find a sword that casts lightning bolts on critical hits, or armor that pushes enemies away when you take damage. A Legendary weapon with 4 chaotic affixes can completely change how you play the game.

## 5. Safe Zones & Boss Arenas

- **Cities (Safe Zones):** Maps are massive. Very rarely, you will stumble into a generated city. The moment you step into a city, all pursuing enemies will instantly drop their aggro and run away. Inside cities, you will find vendors to trade currency for items, allowing you to optimize your build in complete safety.
- **Boss Arenas:** Bosses guard the best loot in the game. They sleep in locked rooms and will ignore you until you step inside. **Warning:** Once you step inside a boss room, the doors lock behind you. You cannot escape until the boss is dead. Winning opens a connected treasure room loaded with powerful loot.

## 6. Progression

Killing monsters grants Experience (XP). When you level up, you receive a Dual Point payout:

1. **Attribute Points:** Used to manually increase your core base stats (e.g., raw Health, Strength).
2. **Skill Points:** Used to unlock powerful new abilities and passive nodes on your Skill Tree.
